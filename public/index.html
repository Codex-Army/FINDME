03 / OSINT TOOLKIT

  • Google Lens — visual matching and reverse image investigation.
  • Google Maps — geographic context and landmark comparison.
  • Google Street View — street-level visual verification where available.
  • OpenStreetMap — roads, place names, and map features.
  • Google Search — searching distinctive sign text and landmarks.
  • EXIF metadata viewers — inspecting metadata when original image files contain it.

Verify through independent sources. A single match is a lead, not proof. Confirm a finding by checking that at least two unrelated sources agree — for example, a sign's wording in a web search, the same junction in Street View, and the road name on OpenStreetMap. Screenshots and re-shared images often have stripped or missing metadata, so EXIF data may be absent and, when present, can be edited; never treat it as proof of location on its own.
